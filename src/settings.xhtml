Abbreviation Helper shows three kinds of tooltips in the reader, each set up in its own section below: 1 · the meaning of an abbreviation, when you point at it; 2 · database links for gene, protein, cell line and chemical names; 3 · where a word is used in the paper, when you press a key over it. All three look alike, and in all three you choose a numbered entry by clicking it or pressing its number. The meaning of an abbreviation, when you point at it. A second line under each meaning: "Defined in this paper", "Probable meaning", "Common term", "From 3 other papers in your library" and so on. When a paper uses an abbreviation without defining it, show the meaning your other papers give it. Learned from the papers you open in the reader and kept on this computer. Forget learned definitions Meanings you define here are always used, even when a paper defines the short form differently. Short form Meaning Add Short forms the plugin should never explain — for wrong detections, such as a supplier name that happens to look like an abbreviation. Short form Ignore Stop ignoring all Lookup links for gene, protein, cell line and chemical names under the pointer: on their own for a word without a meaning, or under a meaning. Database Add or remove databases… The first uses of any word in the paper, with the sentence around each, when you press a key over it. Most useful for an abbreviation without a meaning: its first use often defines it. To go to a use, click it or press its number; the reader's Back button takes you back. The key needs the keyboard focus in the paper: if it does nothing, click the paper once. For all three tooltips. Off by default: the Tools menu holds only actions, and settings live here. Turn it on to get every setting in the menu as well. On macOS the menu closes after each change — a system behaviour for native menus that no plugin can override — so this window is the easier place to change several at once.
